# Building a business with zero money

For Jasmehr, September 2026. Written to be acted on, not admired. Every
recommendation here costs nothing recurring, and the ones that need a
one-time identity step say so.

## The premise, stated honestly

"Build a business autonomously with zero money" has a hard boundary that no
software moves: **the moment money changes hands, a human with a verified
identity has to be on the receiving end.** Every payment processor, every
marketplace, every ad network does KYC. That is regulation, not a missing
feature. So the plan below is built around a different sequence:

1. Build an audience that reads, for free, with the agent doing the drafting.
2. Find out what that audience will pay for, by asking, not guessing.
3. Only then do the one KYC step, for the one thing that has proven demand.

That order is also the one that works. Almost every zero-money business that
survived started as someone with readers who noticed what those readers kept
asking about.

## What you already have that most people do not

- **5,000 connections** who have never been given a reason to read. That is a
  distribution problem, not an audience problem, and distribution problems
  are cheaper to fix.
- **Proof of taste**: 10M+ impressions on Editdreams, built alone. Nobody has
  to take your word that you understand distribution.
- **Shipped software**: VentureAdda, Agent OS, ClipForge. Each is a story and
  a demo, and Agent OS in particular is a genuinely strong portfolio artefact.
- **A drafting system that runs weekly for free** and writes in your voice.
  Most people trying to build an audience have to find the time to write.
  You have to find the time to approve.

## The three candidates, ranked by how little money they need

### 1. Paid newsletter or community for students at your stage
**Cost to start:** zero. Beehiiv or Substack free tier.
**What you would sell:** not information, which is free everywhere, but
curation and honesty about the stage: what placements are actually like, what
projects got interviews, what did not work. The same-stage circle already
follows you for this.
**KYC step:** only when you turn on paid subscriptions, and only after 5 posts
have earned real replies.
**Realistic numbers:** free newsletters at this stage convert 1 to 3 percent
of engaged readers to paid. 500 engaged readers is a handful of paying ones.
That is not income; it is proof. Proof is what the next step needs.

### 2. Done-for-you "build in public" for other student builders
**Cost to start:** zero. You already have the system.
**What you would sell:** Post Studio, operated by you, for people who build
things and cannot write about them. They give you a weekly voice note; they
get drafts in their voice with the gates you already enforce.
**Why it is credible:** you are the case study. Your own LinkedIn going from
dormant to read is the pitch, and it either happens or it does not.
**KYC step:** getting paid. UPI for Indian clients needs no business
registration at small scale, which is the lowest-friction path that exists.
**Realistic numbers:** 3 clients at a modest monthly rate is a real side
income for a student and takes under 4 hours a week with the system doing
the drafting.

### 3. Marketplace take rate (VentureAdda)
**Cost to start:** already built.
**Why it is third:** it needs two sides to show up, and both sides need the
same audience you are only now starting to build. It becomes viable after
1 or 2, not before.

## What the agent does in this plan, and what it does not

**Does, unattended and free:** drafts posts and newsletter issues from your
check-ins and commits every week; keeps the voice consistent as models change
underneath; refuses drafts with no real substance so nothing hollow ships;
drafts outreach notes to people worth knowing; tracks what you rated as
sounding like you and gets closer.

**Does not, and will not:** send anything, post anything, register anything,
accept money, or send connection requests. Every one of those is either a
KYC boundary, a platform rule that risks the account, or a decision that
binds you personally. The agent's job is to make the approve step take five
minutes instead of an evening.

## The next 30 days, concretely

- **Week 1:** Do three check-ins in Post Studio's Me tab. Approve two posts.
  Comment on five posts by people in the two circles. Publish the voice file.
- **Week 2:** Let the Monday routine draft. Post twice. Note which one got a
  reply and rate it "sounds like me".
- **Week 3:** Same. Add the newsletter draft to the second post as a "reply
  if you want more of this".
- **Week 4:** Read the replies. That is the market research. If ten people
  replied to anything, candidate 1 or 2 has a real signal. Then, and only
  then, the KYC step.

## What would make me say stop

If after 8 posts nobody outside your immediate circle has replied, the voice
or the topics are wrong, not the strategy, and the fix is in the check-ins:
say more true things. If after 16 posts the same is true, the audience is not
on LinkedIn, and the same system points at a different channel with a
one-line change.
