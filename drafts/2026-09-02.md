## Newsletter

**Subject**
The outage that forced a real auth fix

**Body**
Firebase's free tier ran out of email sends this week. Sign-in on ventureadda runs on an emailed link, so that quota running out took sign-in down for everyone.

It also took down something less obvious: the same email link was how a student proved their @thapar.edu address, which is what unlocked posting rights. So the outage was actually two outages wearing one cause.

I moved sign-in to Google, which sends no mail and cannot hit a quota. That part was easy. The harder part was verification. A browser can claim any address is linked to an account, and the security rules have no way to check that claim, so I could not just trust whatever the client sent.

I ended up writing a server endpoint that looks at Google's own record of the account: which provider added which address, not what the page says. Only a genuine google.com identity on the right domain counts. It took 6 commits in one day to get that right, and I'm glad the outage forced it, because the old version trusted the browser more than it should have.
