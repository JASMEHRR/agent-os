## Newsletter

**Subject**

The bot that writes this went quiet for 53 days

**Body**

Back in September I built a content agent called agent-os. Its whole job is to turn one weekly note into a LinkedIn post, a newsletter and a Dev.to article, gated by code instead of by my memory.

The gates are specific, not vibes. No em dashes anywhere, because apparently that is the biggest tell. A LinkedIn hook has to work as a complete thought in under 140 characters, since that is roughly where LinkedIn hides the rest behind see more. Every post needs one real number before a draft even gets shown to me. Hashtags are capped between 3 and 5, and the generic ones get rejected outright.

I also made the agents run on their own instead of only when I had the app open, and made the whole thing survive being opened by someone who is not me. Three pull requests landed in one weekend: numbers 16, 17 and 18.

Then I stopped feeding it. 53 days since the last real commit to agent-os, 69 since the last one to ventureadda. The only thing that landed in between was a test checking whether the pipeline still works, not a note about anything I actually did. A system built to not need me did not need me, in the worst possible way: it had nothing to say because I gave it nothing.

**Close**

If you have built yourself something to keep you accountable, tell me how long it has actually been since you used it.
